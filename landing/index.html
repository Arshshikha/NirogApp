Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: August 6, 2026

We want to ensure a transparent learning experience. Below are the terms concerning course cancellation and refund processing times:

1. Course Enrolments

  • Cancellations made within 24 hours of purchase: Eligible for a 100% refund, provided you have accessed less than 10% of the course modules or test series.
  • Cancellations made after 24 hours: Course fees are non-refundable since digital products are immediately accessible upon purchase.
  • Webinars & Live Masterclasses: Eligible for full refund if requested 12 hours before the session starts. No refund is available once the live session has commenced.

2. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund request is approved, the funds will be automatically credited back to your original payment method (bank account/card/UPI) via our payment gateway within 5 to 7 working days.
