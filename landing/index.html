Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

We aim to ensure a transparent booking experience. Below are the terms concerning cancellation fees and refund processing times:

1. Consultation Booking Cancellation

  • Cancellations made 24 hours or more prior to the scheduled slot: Eligible for a 100% refund.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours prior to the scheduled slot: Refund is subject to the doctor's review, or a standard 50% reservation fee might apply.
  • No-shows (where user fails to attend the video session): No refund will be generated.

2. Lab Tests & Diagnostic Bookings

Bookings are eligible for a full refund if cancelled before the lab representative departs to collect samples. No refunds are available after the diagnostic process is complete.

3. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund request is approved, the funds will be automatically credited back to your original payment method (bank account/card/UPI) via our payment gateway within 5 to 7 working days.
